Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026 · Last updated: September 2026

Athlete Unsaid is committed to protecting the privacy of its Members and creating a community in which athletes can speak openly while understanding how their personal information is handled.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, who may receive it, how long we retain it, and the rights you may have in relation to your personal data.

It applies to athleteunsaid.com and the private Athlete Unsaid online community (together, the "Platform").

2. Our Privacy Principles

Athlete Unsaid is built around private and, where chosen by the Member, pseudonymous participation. In particular:

  • We do not sell Member personal data.
  • We do not provide Member profiles or anonymous community activity to advertisers.
  • We do not use anonymous posts to build advertising profiles.
  • We do not currently use advertising pixels or third-party behavioural analytics such as Meta Pixel, Google Analytics or Hotjar on the Platform.

Anonymous to other Members does not mean anonymous to Athlete Unsaid. Where you use the Platform's anonymous participation functionality, your ordinary Member identity is hidden from other Members as described below. Athlete Unsaid retains the technical ability to associate anonymous activity with the underlying Member account for legitimate operational, moderation, security and legal purposes.

3. Who May Use Athlete Unsaid

Athlete Unsaid is intended only for individuals aged 18 or older. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or use the Platform.

We do not knowingly offer Membership to or intentionally collect personal data from individuals under 18 through the registration process.

If we become aware that an account has been created by a person who does not satisfy our age requirements, we may take appropriate steps to restrict or remove the account and associated personal data.

4. Personal Data We Collect

The personal data we process depends on how you use Athlete Unsaid.

4.1 Registration and Account Data

When you register, we collect:

  • email address;
  • username;
  • password or authentication credentials;
  • primary sport;
  • additional sports;
  • competitive or participation level; and
  • role in sport.

A real name is not required as part of the standard Athlete Unsaid registration process.

You may voluntarily add a name or other profile information later through your account settings. Information voluntarily added to your public profile may be visible to other Members.

Passwords are handled through the Platform's authentication system. We do not use your password for purposes unrelated to authentication and account security.

4.2 Public Profile and Ordinary Community Activity

When you participate using your ordinary Member account, other Members may see information including:

  • your username;
  • avatar;
  • primary sport;
  • level;
  • role;
  • content you post; and
  • a name if you have voluntarily added one to your profile and configured it to be visible.

We also process your posts, replies and other community interactions in order to provide and moderate the Platform. You should therefore consider carefully what information you include in your profile or posts.

4.3 Anonymous Community Activity

Athlete Unsaid provides functionality allowing Members to participate without displaying their ordinary Member identity to other Members. When you use this functionality, other Members may see:

  • "Anonymous Athlete";
  • a generic avatar;
  • your primary sport;
  • your level;
  • your post, reply or message; and
  • in some parts of the Platform, a technical anonymous identifier associated with the anonymous identity.

Your ordinary username and any voluntarily supplied real name are not displayed as the author identity through this anonymous functionality.

However, anonymous participation is pseudonymous rather than technically anonymous from Athlete Unsaid's perspective. Our systems retain the ability to associate anonymous activity with the underlying Member account.

Authorized Platform administration may access this association where reasonably necessary for:

  • moderation;
  • investigation of suspected violations of our Terms or Community Standards;
  • protection of Members;
  • fraud or abuse prevention;
  • Platform security;
  • responding to credible safety concerns;
  • enforcing Membership restrictions; or
  • complying with applicable law, court orders or lawful requests from competent authorities.

We do not reveal the identity behind an anonymous contribution to other Members except where legally required or where otherwise permitted by applicable law in circumstances requiring protection of rights or safety.

4.4 Linkability of Anonymous Activity

The anonymous functionality is intended to hide your ordinary Member identity from other Members. It does not guarantee that separate contributions made through the same anonymous identity cannot be recognized as belonging to the same pseudonymous participant.

For example, a technical anonymous identifier may remain consistent or otherwise be recognizable within certain Platform interfaces.

You should also avoid including identifying information in the content of an anonymous post or message if you do not want other Members to infer your identity.

4.5 Private Messages

Where private messaging functionality is available, we process private messages in order to transmit, store and provide those communications.

Depending on Platform permissions, Members may also be able to communicate using an anonymous identity. In that case, the same principles described above for anonymous participation apply. A recipient may see the anonymous identity, relevant sport and level information and a technical anonymous identifier, but not the ordinary Member identity through the anonymous messaging interface.

Private messages are not public community posts. However, they are stored on infrastructure operated for Athlete Unsaid and should not be regarded as end-to-end encrypted communications that are technically inaccessible to the Platform operator.

Authorized access may occur where reasonably necessary for security, moderation, investigation of abuse, legal compliance or protection of Members.

4.6 Payment and Subscription Information

Payments are processed by our payment service provider, Stripe. Stripe processes payment-card and related transaction information necessary to complete and manage your subscription. Athlete Unsaid does not ordinarily receive or store your complete payment-card number.

We may receive and retain information relating to your subscription and transaction, such as:

  • payment status;
  • subscription status;
  • transaction identifiers;
  • billing amount;
  • billing date;
  • payment failure information; and
  • limited payment or billing information made available to merchants by the payment provider.

Payment processing is also subject to Stripe's own privacy practices.

4.7 Technical and Security Data

When you use the Platform, technical information may be generated automatically, including:

  • IP address;
  • login activity;
  • browser and device information;
  • timestamps;
  • session information;
  • security events;
  • server logs; and
  • other technical information necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the Platform.

We use this information primarily for Platform functionality, authentication, security, fraud and abuse prevention, troubleshooting and technical administration.

5. Sensitive Personal Data

Athlete Unsaid is a mental-performance and peer-discussion community, not a healthcare service.

Members may nevertheless voluntarily disclose personal information in posts or messages concerning experiences such as performance anxiety, pressure, confidence, emotional reactions or other personal matters. Depending on its content and applicable law, information voluntarily disclosed by a Member may constitute sensitive or specially protected personal data.

We ask Members not to upload medical records, clinical documentation, diagnoses or unnecessary sensitive personal information to Athlete Unsaid.

Where a Member nevertheless voluntarily submits such information, we may process it to the extent necessary to provide the community functionality, moderate the Platform, protect Members, comply with law and respond appropriately to the content.

Athlete Unsaid is not intended to maintain medical or psychological health records.

6. Why We Use Your Personal Data

We use personal data for the following purposes:

Providing your Membership

To create and maintain your account, authenticate you, provide Platform access, administer your subscription and deliver the services you request.

Operating community features

To display profiles, posts, replies, anonymous contributions and private messages and otherwise provide community functionality.

Providing anonymous participation

To operate the pseudonymous posting and messaging system while retaining the limited internal ability to associate activity with an account where necessary for legitimate moderation, security and legal purposes.

Processing payments

To administer subscriptions, determine payment status, deal with failed payments and maintain appropriate transaction records.

Moderating the community

To investigate reports, enforce our Terms and Community Standards, prevent harassment and abuse and protect the integrity of Athlete Unsaid.

Security and fraud prevention

To secure accounts and infrastructure, identify suspicious activity, prevent unauthorized access and investigate misuse.

Service communications

To send messages necessary to operate your account and Membership, including account, security, payment, moderation and service-related notifications.

Legal and regulatory compliance

To comply with applicable laws, enforce our legal rights, respond to lawful requests and maintain records where legally required.

Improving Athlete Unsaid

We may use aggregated or genuinely de-identified information to understand how the community is used and to improve Athlete Unsaid. We may also identify general themes and insights arising from community discussions – for example, recurring performance challenges discussed by athletes – where those insights are aggregated or de-identified so that they cannot reasonably be linked to an identifiable Member.

We will not use identifiable private community contributions as public testimonials, case studies or marketing material without separate permission.

7. Legal Bases for Processing

Different data-protection laws describe legal bases differently. Where the EU GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we generally rely on the following bases:

Performance of a contract: where processing is necessary to create and administer your Membership, provide Platform functionality, deliver community features or manage your subscription.

Legitimate interests: where processing is reasonably necessary to operate, secure and improve Athlete Unsaid, moderate the community, prevent fraud and abuse, protect Members and enforce our Terms, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.

Legal obligation: where processing is necessary to comply with laws, regulatory requirements, court orders, accounting requirements or other binding legal obligations.

Consent: where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, such as certain marketing communications or particular processing of information voluntarily provided by you.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw that consent as permitted by applicable law. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Where special-category or sensitive personal data is voluntarily provided, additional conditions required under applicable law will be considered where applicable.

If we introduce newsletters or other direct marketing in the future, we will implement any consent or opt-out mechanism required by applicable law and update this Privacy Policy where necessary.

13. How Long We Keep Personal Data

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and for legitimate legal, operational, security, fraud-prevention, accounting and dispute-resolution requirements.

The appropriate retention period depends on the type of information and why it is held. In general:

  • account and Membership information is retained while your account or Membership is active and thereafter where reasonably necessary for legitimate operational or legal purposes;
  • transaction and accounting information may be retained for periods required by tax, accounting or other applicable law;
  • security and technical logs are retained for an appropriate period based on security and operational requirements;
  • community content may be deleted or de-identified following account deletion, subject to technical and legal requirements; and
  • residual copies may remain temporarily in system backups until those backups are overwritten through the normal backup cycle.

We do not retain personal data indefinitely merely because it may be useful in the future.

14. Account Deletion

Members may delete their account directly through the Platform's account settings where this functionality is available.

If self-service deletion is not available for your account, you may request deletion by contacting us at info@kristingrube.com.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Personal data and associated content will be deleted or de-identified, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain for legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes.

Residual copies may remain temporarily in backups until overwritten through our normal backup cycle.

Where applicable law gives you a separate legal right to erasure, that right remains unaffected by the Platform's technical account-deletion functionality.

15. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction.

These measures include controlled administrative access, authentication mechanisms, server and account security measures and limiting access to personal information according to operational need.

No internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security. You should use a strong, unique password and protect your login credentials.

If we become aware of a personal-data breach, we will assess and address it in accordance with applicable law, including applicable notification requirements.

16. Administrative Access

Access to Member-level administrative information is restricted.

At present, Athlete Unsaid is operated with a limited administrative structure and access to Member data is not provided to external developers or webmasters as part of routine Platform operation.

Infrastructure and service providers may have the technical ability to process information to the extent necessary to provide their respective services and subject to applicable contractual and legal obligations.

Administrative access to the relationship between an anonymous identity and the underlying Member account is reserved for legitimate moderation, security, safety and legal purposes.
